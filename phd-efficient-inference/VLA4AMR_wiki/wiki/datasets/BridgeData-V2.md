# BridgeData V2

**Type:** dataset
**Status:** active
**Last updated:** 2026-05-23
**Related:** [[overview]], [[C2-MidLevelActionHead]]

## Summary
Primary SFT corpus. ~15K navigation episodes filtered from 60K total. Closest to AMR locomotion in open datasets. Already in OpenVLA pretraining distribution.

## Curation
- Filter: forward locomotion > 1m, CLIP similarity > 0.3
- Split: 80/10/10
- https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/datasets/bridge_release/
